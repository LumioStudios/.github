## Words from the Founder

Lumio Studios didn’t emerge from a meticulously crafted master plan but rather from a single question—and an impulsive answer.

When I was just 12 years old, a casual conversation with a friend sparked it all. They asked if I could design Minecraft banners and profile pictures. At the time, I had zero experience—no GIMP, no Photoshop, not even a basic design tool installed. Yet, without hesitation, I replied with a confident *yes*.

That spontaneous response—part joke, part curiosity—turned out to be the starting point for everything that followed.

Determined to back up my spur-of-the-moment promise, I plunged into the unknown. I began exploring design methods, tinkering with unfamiliar tools, and learning through trial and error. Little by little, I started piecing together what made visuals work. What initially felt like guesswork became a passion. Designing Minecraft visuals turned into my creative sandbox, where I unwittingly absorbed lessons in composition, color theory, and visual identity.

By 2015, my curiosity expanded into the world of video production. At first, it was just another experiment—figuring out the mechanics of motion, dissecting how timing impacts emotion, and discovering the synergy between sound and imagery. By early 2016, creating videos became a consistent part of my creative routine. With tools like Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects in hand, I began crafting everything from long-form videos to short, punchy edits. This phase unlocked new skills in editing, motion graphics, pacing, and visual storytelling.

Over time, that initial curiosity transformed into discipline. I advanced from basic experiments to streamlined creative workflows, evolving from simple designs to intricate 3D modeling, cinematic rendering, and narrative-rich motion storytelling. What began as an impulsive yes gradually unfolded into Lumio Studios—a name that embodies growth, ambition, and reverence for the art of creation.

Now, at 22 years old, Lumio Studios stands as a testament to that journey: a space born from doing, learning, and embracing imperfection while striving to break creative boundaries. Yet throughout this evolution, one belief has remained unwavering—visuals must have purpose. Design and video are not merely decorative; they are profound languages crafted to communicate meaning, evoke inspiration, and withstand the test of time.

## Tools and Creative Process  

Our approach seamlessly combines artistic intuition with cutting-edge creative tools:  

- **Blockbench**  
  Ideal for crafting expressive and stylized 3D models, emphasizing form, clarity, and character design.  

- **Adobe Photoshop**  
  Essential for concept art development, texture creation, compositing, thumbnails, and overall visual enhancement.  

- **Cinema 4D**  
  Adds depth, dynamic lighting, motion, and a cinematic essence to scenes and renders.  

- **Adobe Premiere Pro**  
  The cornerstone of our video editing process, optimizing both long and short-form content.  

- **Adobe After Effects**  
  Perfect for motion graphics, visual effects, animations, and creating dynamic storytelling experiences.  

These tools amplify our vision, but creativity always takes the lead.
